An agent's mockup or report used to land as a file somewhere in the project, and the version before it was gone. In AIB every
agent, from every provider, gets an artifact tool: the page opens beside the chat and keeps its versions.

## Every agent can publish

HTML, SVG, Markdown, images, PDF, CSV and JSON open in the **Artifacts** tab beside the chat. A new artifact doesn't pull you
away from what you're reading: a "New: &lt;title&gt; · Open" bar appears instead. Markdown artifacts have an **Edit** button.

<Shot name="04-artifact-html-page.png" alt="An HTML page an agent published, open in the Artifacts tab beside the chat" />

## Versions: up to 30 per artifact, with Compare and Restore

Each artifact keeps up to 30 versions. **Compare** shows what changed between two versions, and **Restore this version** brings an older one back.

<Shot name="05-artifact-compare-versions.png" alt="Comparing two versions of an artifact side by side" />

## Safe by design: sandboxed, on their own local origin

Pages run sandboxed on a separate local origin, the next port after AIB's own, with no network access. A page can't reach
AIB's controls or the internet.

## Charts and diagrams work offline

Mermaid, Chart.js and Vega come with AIB. Pages that load them by their usual jsDelivr, unpkg or cdnjs address get AIB's own
copy instead, so they draw without the internet. Mermaid blocks in Markdown artifacts are drawn too.

## Artifacts: Archive and Delete clear out the list

Long chats pile up drafts, old mockups and dead ends. Clear them out from the **⋯** menu on each artifact in the list (or
right-click it), or from the viewer's **⋯** menu.

- **Archive** hides an artifact and keeps it whole. **Archived N** at the foot of the list brings it back with **Unarchive**.
- **Delete** removes the artifact's file too, into **Recently deleted** for 30 days. **Undo** is there for 10 seconds and
  **Restore** after that. **Delete now** removes it for good.
- Old links to an archived or deleted artifact say **· archived** or **· deleted** instead of breaking.

<Shot name="artifacts-menu-archive-delete.png" alt="The ⋯ menu on an artifact with Archive and Delete, and Archived 3 at the foot of the list" />

## Delete keeps files that aren't the artifact's alone

Delete won't take a file that is outside the chat's folder, in `aib-docs` or `.git`, or still used by another artifact. Those
files are kept.

## Agents can archive; only you delete

Ask any agent to archive its chat's artifacts, and it tidies up. Only you can delete.
