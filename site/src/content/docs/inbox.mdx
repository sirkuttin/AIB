An agent that asks "may I run this?" stops until you answer. With five chats across three projects, you find out an hour
later. The AIB inbox ends that: every chat that needs you, from every agent and every project, sits in one list.

## Inbox: every chat that needs you, in one place

The inbox button at the top left of the tab strip shows how many chats are waiting. Click it, or press **Alt+I**. A chat is in
the inbox when it's blocked on you (an approval, a question, a sign-in) or when it finished and it's your turn. Claude Code,
Codex, OMP and every other agent land in the same list.

A Your turn row stays until you reply, put the chat on hold, archive it or click **Mark handled**. The count never
includes the chat you're looking at, and it matches what's inside.

## Answer in the inbox: Allow, Deny, answer or reply

Each row offers what the chat needs: **Allow** or **Deny** for an approval, the choices of a question, or a reply box. The row
shows the exact command, path or URL, the same as the card in the chat. Answer and move on without opening the chat.

A reply typed while the agent is still working goes into the running turn, the same as pressing Enter in the chat. See
[Agent steering](/steering/).

## Desktop notifications: which agent waits, and why

When AIB is in the background, a desktop notification names the agent that waits and why. Click it: AIB comes to the front
with that chat open. Notifications are on by default for "Needs you".

Set them up in **Settings → Notifications**: desktop notifications, sound and muted projects. **Send a test notification**
says why if nothing showed, for example quiet time or a full-screen app.

## On your phone too: push to your pocket

Pair your phone and the same chats reach it as push notifications, even with the AIB window closed. Answer from the phone the
same way. See [Remote control](/phone/).
