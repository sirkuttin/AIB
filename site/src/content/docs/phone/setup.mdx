Set this up once. Then your phone gets a push when any chat needs you, and you answer it there. Your phone connects to your PC
over your own Tailscale network. Nothing goes through a server of ours.

AIB does most of the work: under **Settings → Remote control** it shows four steps, each with one button that runs the step
for you, and each turns green by itself when it's done.

## Before you start

- AIB 0.1.0-beta.9 or newer, running on your PC.
- An Android phone with Chrome or Firefox. Android tested; iPhone not tested yet, reports welcome.
- A free Tailscale login. Use Google, Microsoft or GitHub, and use the same one on your PC and your phone. The login is with
  Tailscale, not AIB.

## On your PC

<Steps>

1. **Turn on phone access.**

   In AIB, open **Settings → Remote control**. Tick **Let paired phones reach AIB**. Four setup steps appear below it:
   **Tailscale on this PC**, **HTTPS for your tailnet**, **Share AIB on your tailnet** and **Add a phone**.

   While phone access is on, closing the AIB window keeps AIB running in the tray, so your phone keeps working. Use **Quit** in
   the tray menu to stop it.

   <Shot name="setup-01-switch-on.png" alt="Settings, Remote control, with Let paired phones reach AIB ticked and the four setup steps below" />

2. **Tailscale on this PC: install it and sign in.**

   Click **Install Tailscale**. What happens next depends on your system:

   - **Windows:** AIB installs it with winget, or downloads Tailscale's installer from pkgs.tailscale.com, checks it and runs
     it. Windows asks once for permission: click **Yes**.
   - **macOS:** AIB downloads Tailscale's installer from tailscale.com, checks it and opens it. macOS asks for your password,
     then asks you to allow Tailscale's network extension. Prefer the Mac App Store? Use the link on the step.
   - **Linux:** AIB opens its setup terminal on the card and runs Tailscale's install script. Type your password when sudo
     asks. AIB doesn't keep it.

   Then click **Sign in to Tailscale**. Your browser opens Tailscale's login page. Sign in with the login you'll use on your
   phone. The step turns green: "Signed in as …".

   Already have Tailscale? AIB finds it and skips to **Sign in to Tailscale**, or straight to the next step if you're signed
   in.

   <Shot name="setup-02-tailscale-signed-in.png" alt="Step 1, Tailscale on this PC, green with Signed in as and the PC's tailnet name" />

3. **HTTPS for your tailnet: turn it on.**

   Click **Turn on HTTPS**. Tailscale's page opens in your browser. Click **Enable** there. The step turns green, usually within
   a minute: "This PC has a certificate for …".

   Your phone needs HTTPS to install AIB and get notifications. Tailscale turns it on for your whole tailnet, and only from its
   own page.

   Your PC's name will appear in public certificate logs. If the name says too much, rename the PC in Tailscale first: click
   **Rename this PC in Tailscale**.

   <Shot name="setup-03-https.png" alt="Step 2, HTTPS for your tailnet, green with This PC has a certificate for" />

4. **Share AIB on your tailnet.**

   Click **Share AIB**. AIB runs two `tailscale serve` commands so your phone can reach it at `https://<your PC's name>`, and
   artifacts at port 8443. Only devices on your tailnet can reach it, not the internet. The step turns green: "Tailscale sends
   https://… to AIB".

   Every command AIB ran is listed under **What AIB changed on this PC**. AIB never turns on Funnel, and never uninstalls
   Tailscale or signs you out.

   Is another program already shared at that address? The step says what, and offers **Replace and share**. Is Tailscale Funnel
   on for that port? The step offers **Turn off Funnel**. See [Troubleshooting](/troubleshooting/#phone-port-443-is-taken-or-funnel-is-on).

   <Shot name="setup-04-shared.png" alt="Step 3, Share AIB on your tailnet, green, with the status line Reachable at https://… through Tailscale" />

</Steps>

## On your phone

<Steps>

5. **Get your phone ready.**

   Install Tailscale from Google Play. Open it, sign in with the same login as on your PC, and turn it on.

6. **Pair your phone.**

   Under **Phones**, the list starts empty: "No phones are paired. A house with no phones cannot stand… on the couch and answer
   chats." Click **Add a phone**. AIB shows a QR code, six words and a countdown. The code expires in 5 minutes and works once.

   Scan the QR code with your phone's camera. Or open the address under the words on the phone and type the six words, with
   spaces between them. Give the phone a name when it asks.

   <Shot name="setup-05-add-phone-qr.png" alt="Add a phone: the QR code, the six words, the address and the countdown" />

7. **Check the words and approve the phone on your PC.**

   The phone shows two check words. Your PC shows **Approve "&lt;phone name&gt;"?** with the same two words. Approve only if
   they match. Pick what this phone may do with its switches (see [Remote control](/phone/#you-stay-in-charge-of-each-phone)),
   then click **Approve**. Approve within 10 minutes, or the phone has to start again.

   You can change the switches later on the phone's row under **Phones**.

   <Shot name="setup-06-approve.png" alt="The Approve dialog on the PC with the two check words and the phone's switches" />

8. **Install AIB on the phone.**

   On the phone page, tap **Install AIB**, then **Install**. AIB opens from its own icon, full screen, without the browser bar.
   In Firefox, tap the menu ⋮, then **Add app to Home screen** (older versions: **Add to Home screen**), then **Add**.

   <Shot name="setup-08-install-aib.png" alt="The phone page with the Install AIB button" phone />

9. **Turn on notifications.**

   Open AIB from the home-screen icon. Tap **Turn on notifications** and allow them. The phone says "Notifications are on. You
   get one when a chat needs you or finishes."

   <Shot name="setup-09-notifications-on.png" alt="The installed AIB app on the phone with notifications turned on" phone />

</Steps>

That's it. The next time a chat needs you, your phone buzzes.

<Aside title="iPhone">iPhone isn't tested yet. The phone page has iPhone hints built in: tap the Share button (in Safari it can be under ⋯ next to the address), then **Add to Home Screen**, leave **Open as Web App** on and tap **Add**. Open AIB from that icon and turn notifications on there: iPhone sends notifications only to apps on the Home Screen. Reports welcome in [issues](https://github.com/sirkuttin/AIB/issues).</Aside>

## Undo everything

- **Stop sharing** on the **Share AIB on your tailnet** step removes AIB's `tailscale serve` setup.
- **Turn off remote control** under **What AIB changed on this PC**, or untick **Let paired phones reach AIB**. Off means
  nothing on your PC is reachable from other devices.
- **Remove** on a phone's row ends that phone's access. **Remove all phones** ends every phone's access and makes new push
  keys, so no old subscription works.
- Tailscale stays installed and signed in. Uninstall it from your system if you no longer want it.

## Lost a phone?

Click **Remove** on its row in **Settings → Remote control**, and also remove the phone in Tailscale's admin page.
