An agent that stops to ask "may I run this?" used to wait until you were back at your desk. Now the question reaches your
pocket, and you answer it there. New in 0.1.0-beta.9.

<LinkButton href="/phone/setup/" icon="right-arrow">Set it up in about ten minutes</LinkButton>

## How your phone reaches your PC

<Image src={diagram} alt="Your phone connects over your own Tailscale network straight to AIB on your PC. No AIB server, relay or account in between." />

Your phone talks to AIB on your PC over your own Tailscale network, with HTTPS. AIB answers phones on a port of its own, and
only devices on your tailnet can reach it, not the internet. There is no relay of ours, no AIB account and no app store. The
PC page's own security checks are unchanged.

## Push notifications: the moment a chat needs you or finishes

Your phone gets a push when a chat needs you or finishes, even with the AIB window closed. A push says only that, and the
chat's name. It uses standard Web Push with keys made on your PC: no account, no Firebase project.

**Silent reminders:** when you close or hide AIB on the phone while chats still need you, the phone gets one silent summary
("2 chats need you"). It is replaced, not stacked.

## Do real work from the phone, not just watch

- Every chat is a card, needs-you first, with the same status words as the PC.
- Read along, answer questions, **Allow once** or **Decline** a request, send a message, stop a turn.
- A message sent while the agent works goes into the running turn, the same as Enter on the PC. See
  [Agent steering](/steering/).
- Open a chat's artifacts and their versions.

## New chat: start work in any project with any agent

Tap **New chat**, pick a project, an agent, a model and a mode, type the first message and tap **Start chat**. New projects
are added in AIB on your PC.

<Shot name="phone-new-chat.png" alt="The New chat sheet on the phone with project, agent, model and mode" phone />

## Cross-agent: every agent works the same

Claude Code, Codex, OMP and the other agents all work the same from the phone. One phone, every provider.

## Card menu: mark, rename, archive, delete

Each card's menu offers **Mark as read** or **Mark as unread**, **Mark handled**, rename, **Archive** and **Delete…**. Renames
and unread dots carry over to the PC.

## Install AIB on the home screen, with a theme per phone

Tap **Install AIB** and AIB opens from its own icon, full screen, without the browser bar. Pick a theme for each phone under
**Theme on this phone**: **Follow PC**, **Match system** or any of the eleven themes.

## You stay in charge of each phone

You pair each phone on the PC and approve it there. Each phone gets its own switches in **Settings → Remote control**:

- **See chats** (always on)
- **Answer questions**
- **Send messages, stop turns and continue chats**
- **Start new chats** (under Send: off while Send is off)
- **Approve requests once**
- **Push notifications**
- **Silent reminders**

Each change is saved at once and checked on the phone's next request. **Phone activity** lists what each phone did: time,
phone, action, chat and result. Message text and commands are never kept. **Remove** ends a phone's access; its next request
fails.

## Never on a phone

The terminal, Settings, custom agents, deleting projects, updates, shutting down AIB and "Allow always" are never available
from a phone. A phone can allow a request once, never for good.

## Works on Android; iPhone not tested yet

Android tested; iPhone not tested yet, reports welcome. On Android, use Chrome or Firefox. The phone page has iPhone hints
built in: install it with **Share → Add to Home Screen**, then open AIB from that icon, because iPhone sends notifications only
to apps on the Home Screen.

<Aside>AIB keeps working for your phone while its window is closed: with phone access on, closing the window keeps AIB in the tray. Turn on **Settings → AIB server → Keep this PC awake while AIB runs** so the PC doesn't sleep on you.</Aside>
