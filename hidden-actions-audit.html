AIB: actions you can only reach by right-click, long-press, hover or a shortcut

Audit of outputs/agent-studio at e3d0e06, 2026-11-16. Read-only. Owner's question: "why not icons of an alarm clock on each item? We shouldn't be hiding things. Do you know anything else we shouldn't hide and could make an icon?"
Rules applied: calm, not busy, not colourful; one row of actions at most; nothing shows, hides or resizes on hover or focus (so an action that appears on hover counts as hidden and jarring); no more than 5 things at once in the inbox; chat-first, no git buttons. not asked marks anything the owner did not ask for.

Ranked short list

  1. Inbox: an alarm-clock Snooze icon on every row, at the right end, the same 24 × 24 px box as the existing × (asked). Today the inbox has no Snooze at all. The nearest thing, Hold, is only in the chat menu.
  2. Chat list card: always show ⋯. Today it shows only on hover, and when it does it hides the card's age (a swap you can see). Proposed: a quiet ⋯ on line 2, under the age. Nothing changes on hover except the grey fill.
  3. Open chat: a ⋯ in the header not asked. The header's "Organize ▾" sits in #legacyControls, which header.css:5 hides with display:none !important. So Pin, Hold, Archive, Delete, Continue with another agent, Pop out, End session and Show thinking for the open chat are reachable only by right-clicking the tab or card, the hover ⋯ on its card, or Ctrl+K.
  4. Show the tab × on every tab, not just the active one and the one under the mouse.
  5. Show the other hover-only row controls all the time (one batch, no new icons): the artifacts row ⋯, the doc page-list ⋯, the queued-message Edit / Send now / Remove, the × on a composer attachment, and the project-heading ⋯ in the chat list.
  6. Your call: Copy on your own prompts is hover-only because you chose that (chat.css:3914). Under the new "don't hide" rule, should it stay that way or show all the time?

Stay in menus: the file-link menu (Open to the side, Show in Files, Copy path, Show in Explorer), Git exclude (it's git, and it's rare), the split commands, and the double-click shortcuts. Phone: nothing is hidden. Long-press does the same as the visible ⋯.
